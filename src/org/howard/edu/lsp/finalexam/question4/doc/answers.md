At the beginning of the semester, I saw AI as mostly a quick way to get 
answers or help finish assignments faster. As the course went on, my 
perspective changed because I started using it more to understand concepts 
instead of just getting solutions. It became more of a learning tool that 
helped me break down difficult topics and fix mistakes in my code.
